# VICE EXCHANGE — Product Requirements Document (PRD)

**Status:** DRAFT — awaiting founder approval  
**Version:** 0.1 | **Prepared:** 2026-11-14  
**Target event:** Nace AI Drexathon | **Primary track:** Swarm Intelligence  
**Product category:** Multiplayer, AI-agent-driven, fictional in-game economy  
**Companion docs:** `VICE_EXCHANGE_REQUIREMENTS.md`, `VICE_EXCHANGE_TECHNICAL_DESIGN.md`

> **One-line pitch:** A living GTA-inspired city where every character has a tradable fictional stock, every NPC is an autonomous decision-maker, and getting captured sends your current character's stock to zero.
>
> **Boundary:** This is a fictional entertainment simulation with **virtual, nonredeemable credits only**. No deposits, withdrawals, cryptocurrency, or real-money trading in the MVP.

## 1. Executive summary

VICE EXCHANGE is a social multiplayer city simulation. Human players complete stylized in-game missions, develop notoriety, evade capture, or become bounty hunters. Their **current character** is represented by an in-game ticker. Other humans and AI trader NPCs can speculate on that ticker using fictional credits. Police, witnesses, informants, traders and other characters decide what to do based on their *own observations and memories*; information propagates through messages. Capturing a wanted character immediately **halts its listing and sets its price to zero**. The player may respawn with a new character and ticker, but the world retains their historical record.

The main innovation is **interlocking agent incentives**: a witness's decision to report, a police agent's decision to investigate, a trader's decision to sell, and a player's choice to hunt or hide can all influence the same market. The system must demonstrate *actual Drex decisions* affecting causal game state, not simply NPC dialogue generated by an LLM.

## 2. Problem / opportunity

Most open-world NPCs are scripted and forgetful. AI game demos often give characters conversational personalities but little agency or lasting consequence. Most market simulations are disconnected from the simulated world. VICE EXCHANGE unifies **memory + autonomous decisions + shared state + price formation + multiplayer** into a visibly interactive system.

The hackathon prompt specifically welcomes **traders in simulated markets** and **NPCs that think for themselves**, with Drex making agent decisions. The project should optimize for a *measurable, live, explainable* swarm demo rather than a giant unfinished game world.

## 3. Audience and jobs to be done

| Persona | Job | Key moment |
|---|---|---|
| Human outlaw | Take fictional missions, build notoriety and survive | My stock climbs after a successful mission, then drops when the city learns I'm being hunted |
| Human hunter | Track wanted characters and claim virtual rewards | Witness reports alter who I can pursue |
| Human trader | Buy/sell fictional character shares | A rumor changes my thesis before the market moves |
| Spectator / judge | Understand how an AI swarm changes the city | I can inspect the actual state, model decision, downstream events and outcome |
| AI NPC | Decide with partial information and distinct incentives | I receive a message, update memory, and choose an allowed action |

## 4. Core gameplay loop

1. Player joins a shared game room and spawns as a character with a unique **ticker**, virtual wallet and a starting share price.
2. Player completes a simple fictional mission (e.g. collect a marked package or grab an objective) to increase notoriety and the character's **reference value**.
3. Nearby NPC witnesses record **only what they see**. They may report, gossip, ignore, or demand additional evidence, determined by Drex from their own state.
4. Traders and the police receive information through explicitly recorded messages, evaluate their options using Drex, and act.
5. The market reprices based on notoriety, survival/capture risk, and recent buy/sell demand; humans can trade using fictional credits.
6. A human hunter or police NPC can capture a wanted character under server-validated proximity/state rules. The ticker immediately drops to **V$0** and trading stops.
7. The game preserves the incident timeline and character record; the human can respawn with a **new** ticker.

## 5. Core product pillars

- **Every action moves the market:** Game events affect a deterministic pricing model; agent buy/sell decisions affect observed sentiment.
- **The city remembers without being omniscient:** Character memories are distinct; rumors, reports, trust, timestamps, and provenance matter.
- **Autonomy with consequences:** Drex returns an allowed action, which the server validates and executes. Failed or missing calls are clearly labeled.
- **Multiplayer in one room:** At least two browsers can play simultaneously, see each other's state and influence the same market.
- **Explainability is part of the game:** Click an NPC or event to inspect its knowledge, options, decision confidence, message chain, and downstream market effect.

## 6. Product scope and releases

### Release H0 — Hackathon submission MVP (hard scope)

**Must ship:**

- One compact **Vice City-inspired** neighborhood, built with original/appropriately licensed assets. A polished 2D top-down view is an approved fallback if 3D threatens completion.
- 2 simultaneously connected human players in one room (4 stretch), keyboard movement and identity labels.
- 6–8 NPCs spanning **witness, police, trader and informant** roles (at least one of each); each has individual state and bounded memory.
- Server-validated **mission completion**, NPC observation, wanted state, pursuit and capture.
- One ticker per *active character*, a price chart, virtual wallet, buy/sell positions and transaction feed.
- Capture instantly freezes the ticker at zero; respawn creates a fresh ticker; prior records persist.
- **Real Drex choices** for all meaningful NPC decisions while the service is healthy. Visible metadata: decision options, chosen action, confidence/probabilities, request id, latency and outcome.
- One live agent-event timeline and one **repeatable, seeded 3–5 minute demo scenario**.
- Baseline comparison: the same scenario under fixed scripted choices vs. Drex, with outcome and latency metrics.

**Important H0 non-goals:** original GTA/reVC engine integration; massive open world; hundreds of agents; production anti-cheat; procedural dialogue; user accounts; cross-room economy; 24/7 servers; real-money securities; sophisticated order books; LLM-driven path planning; full text-to-action autonomy.

### Release H1 — Polished hackathon stretch

- NDI ingestion of a fictional incident bulletin or police procedure PDF; visible citations in at least one NPC decision.
- A judge-controlled incident button, split screen of NPC beliefs, replay/branch comparison.
- AI-generated visuals from properly licensed assets (Adobe bonus only if genuine tool usage is included and evidenced).

### Release V1 — Post-hackathon product

- 3D town and stronger animation, persistent multi-room backend, player accounts, social squads, economy balancing, stronger NPC social graphs, meaningful market making and player reputation across sessions.
- Experimental **reVC adapter** *only after* verifying buildability, redistribution/asset rights, NPC hooks, game-version compatibility and a multiplayer transport. It is **not** a dependency for H0.

## 7. Market rules (proposed; explicit approval item)

The original premise is retained: **more successful crime/notoriety builds an outlaw's value, surviving increases it, and capture makes the character's current stock worthless**. To create strategic trading rather than a guaranteed upward counter, distinguish:

- **Reference value:** rises with fictional mission successes, accumulated notoriety and survival time; never becomes negative.
- **Market price:** reference value adjusted by *estimated capture risk* and capped buy/sell sentiment. It may fall **before** capture if the police are close or a rumor spreads.
- **Capture:** immediate price = 0, trading disabled, all remaining holdings in that ticker have a marked value of V$0. No real-world financial value.
- **Respawn:** new ticker at baseline; past ticker remains a read-only historical record.

**Proposed MVP price formula (tunables, not a financial model):**

```text
reference = 100 + 15*notoriety_points + 60*completed_missions + 8*survival_minutes
survival_factor = clamp(0.95 - 0.007*heat + 0.02*successful_escapes, 0.15, 0.98)
sentiment_factor = clamp(1 + 0.03*net_buy_units_rolling_60s, 0.75, 1.25)
spot_price = captured ? 0 : max(1, round(reference * survival_factor * sentiment_factor))
```

All calculations occur on the authoritative server, with timestamped price ticks in an append-only event log. NPC traders may attempt to buy/sell, but the server enforces budgets, position caps, inventory rules and anti-spam cooldowns. H0 uses a **simulated house counterparty** with transparent rules, not a claim of realistic price discovery or liquidity.

**Decision for approval:** choose **A: always-up-until-capture arcade pricing** or **B: risk-adjusted pricing (recommended)**. The docs below assume B.

## 8. Key screens and UX

1. **Join Room:** nickname, join code, role selection (Outlaw / Hunter / Spectator); no signup for H0.
2. **City View:** recognizable colorful neighborhood, avatars, NPCs, patrol positions, mission marker, wanted/heat indicator. An active spectator has read-only camera controls.
3. **Market Panel:** ticker cards, current price, recent trend, portfolio, Buy/Sell 1-share controls, transaction alerts, historical captured tickers.
4. **Agent Intelligence Panel:** NPC name/role, local beliefs, last sighting, messages received, Drex option list, confidence, chosen action, last API status.
5. **Event Feed:** mission, sighting, witness gossip, report, order, police chase, capture and stock wipeout in causal order.
6. **Demo Director:** one-button seeded reset, trigger an incident, switch scripted-vs-Drex baseline, capture metrics.

**UX design direction:** neon Vice City-inspired palette (aqua/pink/purple) with crisp dark analytics panes, not copyrighted GTA art, logos or assets. The game is the hero; the market and agent causality remain visible without navigating away.

## 10. Success metrics and exit criteria

| Metric | H0 acceptance criterion |
|---|---|
| Multiplayer | Two real browser clients share the same room and agree on player positions/market state |
| Agency | At least 4 NPC roles, 6 live decision cycles from Drex, each producing a validated visible effect |
| Memory | Witness knowledge does not appear in another NPC until a message or observation event occurs |
| Market | A mission changes the reference value; risk/sentiment can affect spot; capture sets spot to exactly zero |
| Trading | Buy/sell changes wallet and holdings atomically; insufficient funds and post-capture orders rejected |
| Capture | One valid capture executes exactly once, archives ticker and creates an auditable event |
| Reproducibility | Seeded reset produces the same initial simulation setup; external model outcomes are logged/replayable |
| Transparency | Real vs fallback/scripted decisions clearly differentiated in UI |
| Robustness | If Drex is unavailable, the match continues safely in labeled fallback mode; API key never exposed |

**Track-specific measurement:** time per decision, model request id, agent event count, collaboration/competition edges, number of trade/price reactions, and change in mission outcomes relative to scripted controls. Report observed numbers, never speculative benchmarks.

## 11. Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Severity | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| ReVC integration consumes deadline | Critical | H0 browser simulation; reVC as optional future adapter |
| AI appears cosmetic | Critical | Actual typed Drex decisions, full traces, and agent-generated downstream events |
| Model latency/rate limits | High | Event-driven calls, bounded concurrency, backpressure, labeled rule fallback, pre-seeded demo |
| Economic exploits/duplicate capture | High | Server authority, serialized financial mutations, idempotency keys, inventory/position limits |
| Everyone knows everything | High | Distinct NPC memory, observation gates, explicit message provenance |
| User confusion about real stocks/money | High | Prominent fictional credits/no-cash-out notice |
| Copyright/licensing | High | Original assets for H0; reVC only with owned GTA assets and legal review before redistribution |
| Overengineering under short deadline | Critical | Prioritize one complete causal loop; degrade from 3D to top-down if needed |
