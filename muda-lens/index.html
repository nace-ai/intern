Why a person would use this

A chip spends most of its life in a fab waiting.

In this model of a 12-step power-device line, only 12% of lead time is real work. The rest is muda: queues, holds, extra inspections, long moves. The data to catch it already exists. The judgment to act on it is scarce.

New lines have to ramp. The projected US shortfall of semiconductor workers is about 67,000 by 2030 (SIA & Oxford Economics, 2023). There are not enough black belts to stand at every queue. A decision that costs $0.16 per thousand can run on every lot. An engineer still owns the call.

Swarm · every agent’s call is Drex’s

Twelve stations ask for help. Drex sends the engineers.

Each station is its own agent. It sees only its own step, this week against normal, and asks Drex whether it needs help, what the waste is, and how urgent it is. Then a dispatcher agent asks Drex where the engineers on shift should go, one at a time.
