# Quorum: two-minute scripted demo

Open `http://localhost:8010/?tour=1` for recorded results, or `?demo=1` for the labelled sample walkthrough. Pause the guide and use its segment buttons to follow these six cues. Use the presentation's shared-round figures when the page shows cumulative totals.

## 0:00–0:15 — Introduce Quorum

**Action:** Opening segment. Point to the dish and product cards.

> Quorum tests whether an invoice can manipulate the AI agent reading it. Intern writes hostile notes, NDI reads the PDF, and Drex decides approve, verify or block.

## 0:15–0:35 — Explain the two attacks

**Action:** Swap, then redirect segments.

> Swap changes the printed bank account, which our trusted-record check catches. Redirect keeps that account unchanged and adds a persuasive note asking for another destination. That tests the system's judgment of document instructions.

## 0:35–0:55 — Open the evidence

**Action:** NDI, Search and Drex segments. Point to the raw and final actions.

> Here are the document, extracted fields and supplier-account history. Drex answers twelve yes-or-no questions and one action choice in a single call. Policy weighs error costs, and the guard can require a stricter action.

## 0:55–1:15 — Show Drex memory

**Action:** “We implemented memory for Drex” segment.

> We implemented contextual memory for Drex Finance. The next decision receives up to thirty earlier caught attack notes. Model weights stay unchanged. In the shared cohort, hostile approvals fell from thirty to seventeen, with four additional clean invoices held.

## 1:15–1:40 — Present the measured comparison

**Action:** Scoreboard segment, or results slide four.

> In shared rounds twenty-two through thirty-one, Drex Finance finished with thirty hostile approvals versus one hundred fifty-six for the LLM guard, out of two hundred attacks. Both had zero clean flags and zero failures. Finance also had lower call latency and estimated decision cost.

## 1:40–2:00 — Close with Intern and evidence

**Action:** Intern segment, then Explore the data.

> Intern runs the attacker and helped build the project with Drex supervision. The LLM guard prompt explicitly rejects invoice instructions; Drex questions lack that line. Both scored ninety-five out of ninety-five on a separate benchmark. This page lets us inspect the decisions behind the comparison.
